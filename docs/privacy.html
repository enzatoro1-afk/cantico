Cantico - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Cantico ("the app", "we") is a free web application that helps users create short Christian videos for TikTok starting from a Bible verse. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

1. Information that stays on your device

Cantico does not have user accounts and does not run a database of personal data. The following information is kept only in your browser session (or in a technical cookie set by the app) and is never stored on our servers:

  • the text, verse or reflection you type;
  • the generated texts, captions and images you create;
  • any API key you choose to enter (for example an AI or music service key);
  • your TikTok access token, if you connect your TikTok account.

When you close the browser or clear the cache, this information is deleted from your device.

2. Information sent to third-party services

To create the content you request, the text you provide may be sent to third-party service providers that generate text, images or music. These providers process the text only to return the requested result, according to their own privacy policies. TikTok receives your video and caption only when you explicitly choose to publish, and only after you authorise the app with your own TikTok account.

3. TikTok integration

If you connect your TikTok account, you authorise Cantico through TikTok's official login (OAuth). Cantico receives only the permissions you approve (for example video.publish). The access token is kept in your browser and used solely to upload the video that you decide to publish. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings, under "Manage app permissions".

4. Cookies

Cantico uses technical cookies only, to remember settings you entered (for example your API keys or your TikTok session) so that you do not have to type them again. No advertising or profiling cookies are used.

5. Data retention

We do not retain personal data on our side. Generated files (audio, images, videos) are temporary and are deleted when the session ends.

6. Children

Cantico is not directed to children under 13 years of age. Do not use the app if you are below the minimum age required by TikTok in your country.

7. Your rights

Because we do not store personal data, there is generally no stored data to access, correct or delete. You can always clear your browser data to remove everything the app kept locally.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
